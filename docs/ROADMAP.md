# Veridex roadmap

## Gate 1 — DETECT

Success = Veridex reliably discovers official publications and catches changed versions without turning page-layout noise into fake regulatory changes.

Alpha sources:
- AUSTRAC latest guidance + news
- APRA publications + prudential standards
- ASIC regulatory tracker

## Gate 2 — UNDERSTAND

Success = for a sampled set of changes, a human reviewer agrees that Veridex correctly identifies the nature of the change, the supported effective date, impacted entity types and source evidence.

## Gate 3 — MATCH

Success = the same change produces materially different applicability results for a bank, an accounting firm and an unrelated software company, with clear reasoning.

## Gate 4 — ACT

Add assignment, due dates, action templates, policy mapping and approval workflow.

## Gate 5 — PROVE

Add evidence objects, immutable event history, exportable audit packages and reviewer sign-off.

## Geographic expansion

The data model is global from day one. Coverage is earned source-by-source:
1. Australia — financial regulation
2. Taiwan — financial regulation
3. Singapore — MAS
4. Then evaluate Japan / EU / US based on paying demand

## Commercial validation

Do not confuse product admiration with demand. Before heavy global expansion:
- interview compliance users,
- run pilots,
- measure time saved and missed-change rate,
- get at least one willingness-to-pay signal.
